PIXEL PILOT

HANGAR AND MUSIC UPDATE

HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to Pixel Pilot! In this game, you must weave your way through the obstacles, using your UP and DOWN or W and S keys. The further you go, the more Pixel Pieces you will get! Use these to buy new spaceships in the hangar! As time goes on, your spaceship will gain more speed. Be careful, and good luck, pilot!

Update II: Hangar and Music Update (4/26/2025)

Gameplay Changes

-Improved the way in which the game increases the speed. Instead of speed increments every X amount of seconds, the gap between speed increments now gets faster as time goes on. This allows the player to get into a sort of groove before things get too fast, will still making for a climactic gameplay.

-Changed the frequency of obstacles.

-Added the abillity of getting in NEW spaceships with DIFFERENT specifications! Click on 'hangar' in the main menu to view and purchase spaceships. Although there is only one alternative spaceship as of this update, WAY more are on the way, with some designs from 8-9ers. It took a lot of programming to enable changing spaceships, so further additions will be faster and easier in the future. If YOU would like to see a spaceship you made in this game, let me know!

-Added in game music. (See Credits) I had to switch to a different file hoster to host MP3 files-- and needless to say this took a long, long time.

-Greatly fixed spaceship hitboxes. Hitboxes are now more accurate and give some leeway to the player.

-Difficulties have been removed because they would allow for more Pixel Pieces after less effort. This will also create a greater incentive to buy spaceships the enhance gameplay in the future.
